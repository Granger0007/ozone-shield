    Already paid, or shared your details?

    Act now. The sooner your bank knows, the better the chance of stopping the money.

    1. Call your bank on 159. It connects you safely to most UK banks. Or use the number on the back of your card.
    2. Report it to Report Fraud on 0300 123 2040 or at reportfraud.police.uk. In Scotland, call Police Scotland on 101.
    3. Change any passwords you shared, and turn on two-step verification.

    Last updated: November 2026  ·  Operated by OZONE Security (sole trader, England & Wales)

    What this service does

    OZONE Shield analyses text messages you paste into the tool for scam indicators using the Claude AI API (Anthropic, PBC). Analysis is performed automatically with no human reviewing your submitted messages. Results are advisory only — they are not a substitute for professional advice and should not be your sole basis for any financial or legal decision.

    Automated decision-making

    All scam verdicts are generated entirely by artificial intelligence. This is disclosed on every result. You have the right to query any automated verdict that affects you. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

    Data we collect and why

    • Message content you paste: Transmitted to the Claude AI API (Anthropic, PBC) for analysis. OZONE Shield does not store your message content after the result is returned.
    • Server logs: Cloudflare, Inc. (our infrastructure provider) automatically logs IP addresses and request metadata for security and uptime. Governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
    • Bot check: Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a real person is using the page, to stop automated abuse of the service. It runs in your browser and is provided by Cloudflare, Inc.
    • Game progress: Spot the Scam saves your points, level and badges only in your browser's storage on your device. It is never sent to us, and you can reset it in the game at any time.
    • No tracking: We use no analytics cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party tracking scripts.

    Lawful basis

    Message content is processed under legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) for the purpose of fraud detection and scam prevention. We do not retain your message content after analysis, and the processing directly serves your own safety interest — the core factors a legitimate interests assessment weighs.

    Third-party processors

    • Anthropic PBC — Claude AI API, message analysis. US-based; UK data transfers governed by Standard Contractual Clauses. See Anthropic Privacy Policy.
    • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting, edge compute, and serverless infrastructure. US-based. See Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

    Your rights under UK GDPR

    Because OZONE Shield does not store your message content, most data subject rights (access, correction, deletion) apply to Cloudflare's server logs rather than to OZONE Shield directly. You may contact us with any data query and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

    Accuracy & limitations

    AI scam detection is probabilistic. OZONE Shield will sometimes flag legitimate messages (false positives) or miss actual scams (false negatives). Always verify suspicious messages through official channels before taking action. We accept no liability for decisions made based solely on OZONE Shield verdicts.
