    What this service does

    OZONE Shield analyses text messages you paste into the tool for scam indicators using the Claude AI API (Anthropic PBC). Analysis is performed automatically with no human reviewing your submitted messages. Results are advisory only. They are not a substitute for professional advice and should not be your only basis for any financial or legal decision.

    Automated decision-making

    All scam verdicts are generated entirely by artificial intelligence. This is disclosed on every result. You can query any automated verdict that affects you. Contact us at the address below and we will reply within one month.

    Data we collect and why

    • Message content you paste: sent to the Claude AI API (Anthropic PBC) for analysis. OZONE Shield does not store your message content after the result is returned. Anthropic keeps API inputs and outputs for up to 30 days and then deletes them, unless the law requires it to keep them longer or they are flagged for breaking its usage policy.
    • Server logs: Cloudflare, Inc. (our infrastructure provider) automatically logs IP addresses and request metadata for security and uptime. Governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
    • Bot check: Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a real person is using the page, to stop automated abuse of the service. It runs in your browser and is provided by Cloudflare, Inc.
    • Game progress: Spot the Scam saves your points, level and badges only in your browser's storage on your device. It is never sent to us, and you can reset it in the game at any time.
    • No tracking: We use no analytics cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party tracking scripts.

    Lawful basis

    Message content is processed under legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) for the purpose of fraud detection and scam prevention. We do not retain your message content after analysis, and the processing directly serves your own safety. These are the core factors a legitimate interests assessment weighs.

    Third-party processors

    • Anthropic PBC: Claude AI API, for message analysis. Based in the US. Anthropic protects transfers from the UK with adequacy decisions or the UK-approved standard contractual clauses, and its commercial terms do not allow it to train models on content sent through its API. See the Anthropic Privacy Policy.
    • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting, edge compute and serverless infrastructure. Based in the US. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

    Your rights under UK GDPR

    Because OZONE Shield does not store your message content, most data subject rights (access, correction, deletion) apply to Cloudflare's server logs rather than to OZONE Shield directly. You may contact us with any data query and we will reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

    Accuracy & limitations

    AI scam detection is probabilistic. OZONE Shield will sometimes flag legitimate messages (false positives) or miss actual scams (false negatives). Always verify suspicious messages through official channels before taking action. We accept no liability for decisions made based solely on OZONE Shield verdicts.
