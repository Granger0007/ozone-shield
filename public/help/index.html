Get help

Get help, fast.

If you've sent money or shared your details, the first hour matters most. Here's exactly what to do, and who can help.

Already paid, or shared your details?

Act now. The sooner your bank knows, the better the chance of stopping the money.

  1. 1

    Call your bank on 159

    159 connects you safely and directly to your bank. It works for more than 99% of UK retail bank current accounts. Or call the number on the back of your card.

  2. 2

    Report it

    In England, Wales and Northern Ireland, contact Report Fraud on 0300 123 2040. In Scotland, call Police Scotland on 101.

  3. 3

    Secure your accounts

    Change any passwords you shared, and turn on two-step verification.
