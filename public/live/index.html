What does “source” mean?

A source country is where the attacking device's internet address is registered, not where the criminal sits. Attackers often borrow hacked computers or rented servers in other countries. The figures are each country's share of all layer 7 attacks (malicious requests to websites) that Cloudflare blocked.

What to do about it

Turn on automatic updates for your phone, computer, browser and router. Most of these flaws already have a fix: the attacks work on devices that haven't installed it yet.
